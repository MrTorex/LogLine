# Code of Conduct

Our rules are simple: we care about code, collaboration, and results — not your background or personal identity.

### 1. Focus on the Project
We welcome anyone who brings value to this repository. Discussions should revolve around the work: architecture, code quality, bug fixes, and documentation. Keep irrelevant drama out of issues and pull requests.

### 2. Pragmatism Over Sensitivity
- Technical critique of your code is not a personal attack. Separate your ego from your pull requests.
- Don't get offended over trivial phrasing or differing worldviews.
- English is not everyone's native language. Never shame anyone for typos, broken grammar, or awkward phrasing. What matters is being understood and getting things done.

### 3. Don't Be a Pain
Zero tolerance for actual destructive behavior:
- Spamming, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Outright insults, harassment, and personal attacks.
- Refusing to listen to maintainer feedback while pushing low-effort noise.

### Enforcement
Maintainers have full discretion to close threads, reject PRs, or ban users who waste community time with drama instead of building things. Be reasonable, act like an adult, and make useful contributions.
